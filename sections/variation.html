9  Variation

10 Take home messages

  • Methodology: Learn how to draw regional dialect boundaries and recognize the methodological challenges involved.
  • Descriptive linguistics: Understand that every dialect is rule-governed and learn to make generalizations about them.
  • Dialect features: Analyze dialects using features from different linguistic levels — lexical, phonological, morphological, and syntactic.
  • Analytical tools: Practice phonetic transcription and generalization-making using class-taught methods.
  • Dialect diversity: Gain exposure to dialects different from your own.
